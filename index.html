About

Dedicated to better kidney health

Dr Dev Jegatheesan is a Consultant Nephrologist and transplant physician with the Queensland Kidney Transplant Service at Princess Alexandra Hospital, and consults privately at Greenslopes Private Hospital. He completed his BSc and MBBS at The University of Queensland, followed by a kidney transplant fellowship at Princess Alexandra Hospital in 2018.

His transplant work covers assessment, acute inpatient and peri-operative care, waitlist management and long-term follow-up of recipients. Alongside this he manages patients across the full spectrum of kidney disease, and welcomes referrals from general practitioners, specialists and emergency departments — including phone advice where that's the quickest way to help.

Clinical interests

  • Kidney transplantation
  • Diabetic kidney disease
  • Hypertension
  • Glomerulonephritis
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Haemodialysis
  • Peritoneal dialysis

Teaching & research

  • Lecturer and PhD candidate at The University of Queensland, with clinical coaching and examination of medical students
  • Accredited advanced nephrology trainee supervisor with the Royal Australasian College of Physicians
  • Research into physical fitness in kidney disease, patient-centred care, and the interplay between cardiovascular and kidney disease
